How games work

Circuit has unlimited puzzles in six modes, from 3×3 to 10×10. Every mode and size keeps its own board. Use the question mark inside Circuit for its rules and controls.

The word and other logic games offer Daily puzzles: the same puzzle for everyone, refreshed at midnight in London. Free play gives you extra puzzles with separate progress and no effect on your daily streak.

Spider has shuffled deals. Sudoku scans and solves the puzzle you provide. Progress stays in this browser.
