Daily & Free play

Daily is the same puzzle for everyone that day. A fresh puzzle arrives at midnight in London. Each game, and each Circuit variant, keeps its own daily progress and results in this browser.

Free play gives you extra puzzles at your own pace. It keeps your daily progress separate and does not affect your daily streak.

Spider has shuffled deals rather than a daily puzzle. Sudoku scans and solves the puzzle you provide.
